> **Here to make a difference in your future.**  
> A privacy-first shield for a student's digital footprint.

Guardify bridges the gap between teen online privacy and parental peace of mind. Instead of acting like punitive spyware, Guardify uses real-time AI to flag risky draft posts and photo background details *before* they go live—protecting students' college and career opportunities while keeping them rewarded through a gamified "Safe to Earn" ecosystem.

## Key Features

- **FootprintLens AI:** Real-time contextual analysis that flags sensitive text phrasing and risky background objects (like school IDs or house keys) before a post is published.
- **"Safe to Earn" Rewards:** A gamified incentive system where students earn points for high safety scores, redeemable for Roblox, Chipotle, and Starbucks gift cards.
- **Zero-Data Retention:** 100% privacy-first scanning architecture. No private chats are monitored, and no personal data is stored on servers.
- **Parent Insight Dashboard:** Offers parents peace of mind without invading their teen's private messaging.


This is a scripted design prototype for a pitch competition, not a production app: data is local, and the AI voice is represented, not wired up.
